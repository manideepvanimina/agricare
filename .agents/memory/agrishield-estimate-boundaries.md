---
name: AgriShield estimate boundaries
description: Product rules for simulated crop-health reports, weather, chemical advice, and source claims.
---

Keep image analysis and weather explicitly labeled as simulated. Do not present estimates as facts, prescribe chemical names or doses, or invent authoritative sources. Verify sources and describe only what they actually support.

**Why:** The product owner explicitly set these boundaries for AgriShield AI.

**How to apply:** Keep limitations visible in scan reports, risk outlooks, weather displays, and anything users can print or share.
