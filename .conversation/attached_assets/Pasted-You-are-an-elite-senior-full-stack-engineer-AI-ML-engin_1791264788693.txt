# 1. CORE PROBLEM

Farmers frequently discover crop diseases only after visible symptoms have spread significantly.

Most existing solutions stop at:

> “This is Disease X.”

AgriShield AI must go much further:

> 📸 What happened?
> → 🧠 Why did it happen?
> → 💊 What should I do now?
> → 🔮 What could happen next?
> → 🚨 When should I act?

The application should help farmers make faster, safer, evidence-based decisions.

IMPORTANT:
Never present AI predictions as guaranteed facts.
Clearly label predictions as estimates/risk assessments and encourage users to verify serious agricultural decisions with qualified agricultural experts/local agricultural officers.
